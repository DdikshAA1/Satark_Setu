{{ 'परियोजना मिशन एवं समस्या विवरण (Problem Statement)' if current_lang == 'hi' else 'Project Mission & Problem Statement' }}

"How might technology help individuals and small businesses recognise, prevent and navigate digital payment fraud?"

With the rapid expansion of UPI and digital banking in India, millions of first-time users, senior citizens, shopkeepers, and street vendors are targeted by deceptive tactics: fake KYC suspension deadlines, UPI PIN-to-receive traps, deceptive collect requests, electricity disconnection threats, and digital arrest intimidations.

Satark Setu bridges this cybersecurity divide through an intuitive companion that moves beyond black-box risk scores into transparent, compassionate, and actionable guidance.

{{ 'केंद्रीय नवाचार: नेक्स्ट बेस्ट एक्शन इंजन' if current_lang == 'hi' else 'Core Innovation: Next Best Action Engine' }}

Most conventional scam checkers merely return a percentage or generic red warning, leaving the victim in panic. Satark Setu's Next Best Action Engine decomposes every threat across four structured dimensions:

1. Why This is Suspicious Plain-language explanation of deceptive psychological tricks without technical jargon.
2. What to AVOID Doing Concrete traps not to fall for (e.g., entering PIN to receive money, clicking links).
3. Immediate Actions Calm, safe steps to verify authentic status via official banking apps or branch visits.
4. Recovery Protocol Golden Hour actions if funds were debited: 1930 Helpline, bank card freeze, cybercrime.gov.in.

{{ 'प्रौद्योगिकी एवं वास्तुकला' if current_lang == 'hi' else 'Technology Stack & Architecture' }}

  • Frontend: Semantic HTML5, Human-Designed Fintech CSS (Deep Navy `#142B3A`, Warm White `#FAFAF7`, Teal `#2E8B78`), Lucide Icons, Chart.js.
  • Backend: Python 3, Flask modular routing architecture, parameter-driven security headers.
  • AI & NLP: Scikit-Learn TF-IDF Vectorizer + Logistic Regression classifier calibrated on Indian financial fraud corpus, combined with an explainable heuristic rule engine.
  • Database: SQLite with parameterized queries and strict privacy redaction (automatic masking of OTPs, account numbers, and phone numbers).
  • Bilingual Localization: Complete English and simple, respectful Hindi for all modules, categories, and guidance plans.
